Build a premium multi-page website for C. Sethia & Co., an industrial and HVAC distribution company based in Kolkata, India.

This is a B2B informational website, not an e-commerce platform. The primary objective is to establish credibility, showcase our product portfolio and authorised brands, and generate business enquiries from procurement managers, EPC contractors, MEP consultants, builders, developers, hospitals, factories, and industrial facilities.

The website should feel modern, premium, and highly professional while reflecting the trust, experience, and legacy of a company established in 1941.

Take strong inspiration from the content flow, navigation, and product presentation of https://tritonprocess.com/, but create a completely original design that represents C. Sethia & Co.’s own identity.

Please generate the entire website in one go, including all pages, layouts, content, navigation, imagery placement, and responsive design.
